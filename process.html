How AI Implementation Works, Step by Step

A proven 4-step process for AI adoption UK SMEs can follow. From "we know AI matters" to "AI is working in our business." No black boxes. No surprise bills.

The 4D Methodology

Discover · Diagnose · Design · Deliver. A methodology refined through enterprise AI projects, adapted for SME timelines and budgets.

1

Discover

Learn your business inside out. No assumptions.

2

Diagnose

Find real AI opportunities. Honest about what won't work.

3

Design

Architect the solution. Clear roadmap before building.

4

Deliver

Build, integrate, train, support. You own the result.

Step 1 · Week 1

Discover, We Learn Your Business

Every engagement starts with understanding your business, not our technology. We don't walk in with a template, we build the picture from scratch.

  • ✓ Interviews with leadership and key team members
  • ✓ Audit of your current tech stack and software ecosystem
  • ✓ Workflow mapping, how work actually gets done (not how it's documented)
  • ✓ Data landscape review, what data you have, where it lives, quality
  • ✓ Team readiness assessment, skills, attitudes, concerns
  • ✓ Business goals and constraints, what success looks like for you

Output: A baseline understanding document that everything else builds on. You review and approve it before we move to diagnosis.

Step 2 · Weeks 1–2

Diagnose, We Find Real Opportunities

Using what we learned in Discover, we map your workflows against AI capabilities to find where the real value is, and where it isn't.

  • ✓ Workflow-by-workflow AI opportunity assessment
  • ✓ ROI estimates for each opportunity (time saved, cost reduced, revenue gained)
  • ✓ Difficulty and feasibility scoring, what's easy, what's hard, what's not worth it
  • ✓ Quick wins identified (things you can act on in days, not months)
  • ✓ Compliance and data risk assessment for each opportunity
  • ✓ Honest "don't bother" list, things that sound good but won't deliver

Output: A prioritised AI Opportunity Report with ROI estimates. This is the core deliverable of the AI Opportunity Audit.

Step 3 · Weeks 2–4

Design, We Architect the Solution

For opportunities worth pursuing, we design the full solution: which tools, how they connect, and how we roll them out without disrupting your business.

  • ✓ Tool selection, off-the-shelf first, custom only when necessary
  • ✓ Build vs buy analysis for each component
  • ✓ Integration architecture, how AI connects to your existing tools
  • ✓ Data flow design, where data moves, how it's protected
  • ✓ 90-day implementation roadmap with clear milestones
  • ✓ Team training plan, what skills, who needs them, when
  • ✓ Success metrics, how we'll measure impact

Output: A complete AI Strategy & Roadmap document. This is the core deliverable of the Strategy engagement.

Step 4 · Weeks 4–12

Deliver, We Build, Integrate, Train

This is where most consultants stop. We don't. We implement the plan, connect everything to your tools, train your people, and stay for 30 days after launch.

  • ✓ AI system configuration and setup
  • ✓ API integrations with your existing tools (CRM, accounting, comms, etc.)
  • ✓ Workflow automation setup
  • ✓ Security and compliance implementation (GDPR, data residency)
  • ✓ Team training sessions (group + 1:1)
  • ✓ Full documentation, system docs, user guides, troubleshooting
  • ✓ 30-day post-launch support, we're there when things go live
  • ✓ Performance monitoring setup and handover

Output: Working AI systems, trained team, full documentation, and self-sufficiency. This is the Implementation engagement.

Typical Timeline

From first call to working AI systems. Every project is different, but here's what the journey typically looks like.

Day 0

Free 30-min discovery call. We discuss your business and whether AI makes sense.

Weeks 1–2

AI Opportunity Audit. Discover + Diagnose. You get your prioritised opportunity report.

Weeks 3–5

Strategy & Roadmap. Design phase. You get your 90-day implementation plan.

Weeks 6–12

Implementation. Deliver phase. Working AI systems, trained team, 30-day support.

You can stop at any stage. The audit is self-contained. The strategy is self-contained. No lock-in.

Why This Process Works for SMEs

It's designed around the realities of running a 10–250 person business, not enterprise workflows.

De-risks Investment

The £750 audit tells you whether AI is worth it before you spend £15,000. You make informed decisions at each stage, not blind leaps.

Stoppable at Any Point

Each phase delivers standalone value. If the audit shows AI isn't right for you yet, you've spent £750, not £30,000. No pressure to continue.

Built for Real Teams

Your team isn't a data science department, and that's fine. The process works with the skills you have and builds the skills you need.
