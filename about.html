I started ajairu to fix that. Same enterprise-grade thinking about AI strategy, data governance, and implementation discipline, but packaged for SME budgets, timelines, and realities. Fixed-price engagements. Plain English. Practical AI for business, not slideware.

My Approach to AI Consulting

Three principles that guide every engagement.

Outcomes Over Technology

I don't lead with AI. I lead with your business problems. AI is a tool, not a strategy. If a spreadsheet solves your problem better than an AI system, I'll tell you that. The goal is impact, hours saved, costs reduced, revenue grown, not technology deployed.

Capability Over Dependency

Too many consultants build systems that only they can maintain. I do the opposite: every engagement includes training, documentation, and handover designed to make your team self-sufficient. You should be able to run and extend what we build without me in the room.

Honesty Over Hype

AI can't do everything. Some problems aren't AI problems. Some businesses aren't ready yet. I'll tell you that, clearly and early. The £750 audit exists specifically to find out whether AI makes sense for you before you spend £15,000 finding out the hard way.

Boundaries

What I Don't Do

Being clear about what I don't do is as important as what I do. It saves both of us time.

  • ✗ Slide-only strategy. If you want a 200-slide deck and nothing implemented, I'm not your person. I design for execution.
  • ✗ Day-rate open-ended billing. You'll never get a "surprise" invoice. Fixed price, agreed upfront.
  • ✗ Hype-driven recommendations. I won't recommend AI where a simpler tool works better. Technology choice follows the problem, not the other way around.
  • ✗ Dependency-creating systems. If the system only works when I'm around, I've failed. Self-sufficiency is the deliverable.
  • ✗ Ignoring compliance. GDPR, data residency, and security aren't afterthoughts. They're designed in from day one.
