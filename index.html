Questions UK SMEs Ask Us

Straight answers to the most common concerns about AI consulting for small businesses.

How much does AI consulting cost for a small business? +

Our AI Opportunity Audit starts at £750 and gives you a clear picture of where AI can help. Full strategy engagements run £5,000–£15,000, and implementation projects start at £8,000. All engagements are fixed-price with no open-ended billing. We also offer a Fractional CAIO service at £2,000–£5,000/month for ongoing AI leadership.

Do we need technical staff to work with you? +

No. We work with businesses at any level of AI maturity. Our process is jargon-free and we build your team's capability so they can manage AI tools independently after the engagement ends. 75% of UK businesses say they need training and education, that's built into everything we do.

Is our data safe when using AI tools? +

Yes. We take a security-first approach with UK data residency, GDPR compliance, and enterprise-grade data governance built into every engagement. We recommend tools that keep your data within the UK/EU where possible, and we never use your data to train public models.

How long does an AI implementation take? +

An AI Opportunity Audit takes 1–2 weeks. A strategy and roadmap engagement takes 3–5 weeks. Implementation projects typically run 6–12 weeks depending on complexity, with 30 days of post-launch support included. We always start with the audit, it de-risks everything that follows.

What size businesses do you work with? +

We specialise in UK SMEs with 10–250 employees. This is where we see the biggest gap, businesses that are too large for generic advice but too small for Big Four consultancy fees. If you're in that range and you know AI matters but don't know where to start, we're built for you.

What if AI isn't right for our business? +

Then the audit will tell you that, honestly. Not every business needs AI right now, and not every problem is an AI problem. The £750 audit is designed to find out if there's a genuine ROI before you commit to anything larger. Sometimes the most valuable recommendation is "not yet."
